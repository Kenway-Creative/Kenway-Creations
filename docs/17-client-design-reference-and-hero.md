# The Horse — Lee's design reference and homepage wording

**Client feedback received:** 29 October 2026.

## Reference site

https://www.theroyaloaklongwhatton.co.uk/

Lee and the team like The Royal Oak, Long Whatton website. Treat it as **directional inspiration**, not a template to copy.

Relevant observed patterns:
- Clear restaurant and bedroom booking calls to action.
- Prominent genuine venue/food imagery and visual galleries.
- Distinct food, drinks, accommodation and events sections.
- Simple menu and contact navigation.
- Useful practical information such as opening hours and directions.

Apply these principles to The Horse's existing warm, editorial design. Do not copy Royal Oak copy, photography, branding, awards, ratings, specific amenities or layouts wholesale. The Horse's details must come from Lee's approved content.

## Approved homepage opening wording

Replace the original headline and introductory paragraph with **exactly** three stacked words, without additional punctuation:

Bar

Restaurant

Rooms

Keep the Book a Table and Explore Our Menus buttons directly beneath the statement. The rest of the homepage continues to explain the three areas and present genuine venue photography.
